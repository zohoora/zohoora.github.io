Dana privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Dana is a personal, self-hosted application. It is used only by its owner, on hardware the owner controls.

What Dana accesses

When the owner connects a Google account, Dana requests read-only access to Gmail, Google Calendar events and Google Drive. It never sends, modifies or deletes anything in those services.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is copied into the owner's private database on the owner's own computer, to power the owner's personal search and notes.
  • Text may be sent to AI model providers for embedding and summarization on the owner's behalf. It is not used for advertising.
  • Data is never sold, and never shared with anyone other than the owner.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Dana's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Copied data is deleted by the owner from their own database.
